Step by step

How do we plant?

Six simple actions that turn tiny seeds into healthy plants. Use the buttons to hear each step in English.

Step 01
🧤

Prepare the soil

First, we clean the garden area, remove weeds, and loosen the soil so the roots can grow.

Step 02
🕳️

Make small holes

Next, we make small holes in the soil, leaving enough space between them.

Step 03
🌰

Plant the seeds

Then, we place the seeds into the holes and cover them gently with soil.

Step 04
💧

Water carefully

After planting, we add enough water to keep the soil moist, but not flooded.

Step 05
☀️

Give light and care

We check that the plants get the right amount of sunlight and remove new weeds.

Step 06
🌿

Watch them grow

Finally, we observe the plants, measure their progress, and learn about nature.
